02Class Lore

Necromancers turn to dark magic to gain power over the dead and the living. They study bodies, souls, curses and life force: some command the undead, others suppress the will of their enemies or turn their life into a source of their own power. For the necromancer, death is the subject of study and the basis of his art.

The path to this art varies. Someone learns necromancy from a mentor and from ancient books, like a wizard. Some people are born with a gift and only later learn to understand and control it. But even innate strength requires knowledge: books, training and constant practice help turn the first manifestations of a gift into conscious mastery.

To raise the skeleton, the necromancer uses the remains of the deceased creature and breathes into them a particle of his own power. Dark magic moves the bones and binds the servant to the will of the creator. The mere presence of a corpse is not enough: the necromancer needs to empower it and maintain control over it.

This power is limited. Each raised servant takes up part of the necromancer's capabilities, and even many suitable bodies do not allow you to create an entire army at once. Undead limit reflects how many servants and of what complexity the necromancer is able to simultaneously keep under control. Ghostly creatures and multi-body chimeras require more control than a simple skeleton. To subjugate more dead people, he has to improve his control and hone his art for years. In the game rules, this increase in skill is expressed in increasing the level and acquiring new skills.

Necromancers are usually feared: revived bodies, curses and power over life cause distrust even of those who do not wish harm to others. However, the attitude towards necromancy depends on the world and the decision of the master. It may be persecuted, tolerated under certain conditions, or accepted as dark magical art. The origin of the gift does not determine the actions of the necromancer - how he uses his power remains his choice.

A necromancer rarely learns completely new methods of attack. Instead, over the years he has been perfecting familiar magic: the old touch becomes more dangerous, the discharge more powerful, and the bone blades more dense and deadly. His servants grow in the same way: skeletons receive reinforced bones and bone armor, short swords are replaced by long ones, and the flesh of ghouls becomes more durable. Increasing the level expresses the depth of mastery of an already familiar power.

School of the Dark Arts develops personal offensive magic: the necromancer turns darkness into whips, flames and piercing spears. This direction masters new attacks more often than others, but also strengthens old techniques with experience. Other schools focus on curses, subduing the dead, or working with souls.

At high levels, the necromancer learns to share power with a dead general, revive rune skeleton mages, and fuse matter with ancient nexus cores. The general strengthens the undead system, the magicians receive their own limited reserves of spells, and the golem requires deep knowledge of the two mastered forms and long work. Even this skill is limited by the number of control spots: complex creatures take up more space rather than form a free army.

At the pinnacle of mastery, Greater Necromancy strengthens all previous techniques and creations. The necromancer is able to return a willing rational soul, raise the skeleton of a dragon, and at level 20 turn into a lich. His immortality is tied to a separate phylactery and does not override the limits of control or vulnerability of servants.

03Backgrounds

Three background stories for a necromancer character. Each grants skills, proficiencies, starting equipment, and a trait.

Cemetery caretakerBackground

You buried the dead, tended the graves, and noticed things that others chose not to see. One day the dead man responded to your words - or you yourself made him rise.

  • Skills: Religion, Medicine.
  • Possessions: carpenter's tools and one language to choose from.
  • Equipment: shovel, work clothes, funeral amulet, carpenter's tools, 10 gp.

Feature: “Familiar with the last path.” You understand funeral customs and burial arrangements. Among gravediggers, undertakers and caretakers you can usually find accommodations, simple work and information about recent funerals. The attitude towards your necromancy is determined separately.

Additional feature. When you choose this background, you also get the trait "Grave Excerpt".

Disciple of the Forbidden ArchiveBackground

You studied notes on souls, curses, and death magic. Perhaps your mentor taught you secretly. Perhaps it was the books that were found that helped to understand the innate gift.

  • Skills: Magic, Investigation.
  • Possessions: calligrapher's supplies and one language to choose from.
  • Equipment: research notebook, calligrapher's supplies, fragment of a forbidden treatise, ordinary clothes, 10 gp.

Feature: “Trace of Lost Knowledge.” If you don't know about necromancy or a curse, you can usually figure out where to look for it: in which archive, from which scientist, or in whose collection. Gaining access may require a service, a fee, or a separate adventure.

Additional feature. When you select this background you also get one trait to choose from: "Bone Master" or "Unbreakable Bond". The background gives only one of these two traits.

Field doctorBackground

You tried to save people amid blood and destruction. But medicine too often proved powerless. Necromancy became the next step - out of compassion, despair, or the desire to overcome one's own helplessness.

  • Skills: Medicine, Insight.
  • Possessions: herbalist kit and one language to choose from.
  • Equipment: healer's kit, herbalist's kit, memorial sign of the deceased patient, travel clothing, 5 gp.

Feature: “Among the Wounded.” You know the hospital procedures and know how to organize assistance to the victims. Doctors and military medics are usually willing to exchange information or provide simple shelter in exchange for work. This feature does not restore hit points by itself.

08Level 3 Features

Sacrifice on the boneslevel 3 · 1 CE · Reaction · 120 feet

When you have to take damage, you can reaction spend 1 CE and transfer it to one of your servants within 120 feet, including Bone Puppet, Bone Hound, Ghosts and Chimeras. There is no need to see the servant.

Transfer limit per reaction: levels 3–4 — 15 damage, 5–10 — 25, 11–16 — 40, 17–20 — 60. Damage above the limit remains with you; the transferred portion is doubled for the servant according to the rules below.

Calculation procedure:

  1. Determine the damage you would take after your resistances and other reductions.
  2. Separate part of the damage no more than the transfer limit; you get the rest. The chosen servant receives twice the transferred portion. The damage suffered cannot be reduced by resistances or other abilities of the servant.
  3. If the servant is destroyed, divide the remaining damage in half, rounding down. You get this remainder; It cannot be reduced or moved again.

Example: you should be dealt 12 damage. You transfer it to a skeleton with 10 hit points. The skeleton receives 24 damage: 10 destroy it, leaving 14. you get 7 damage.

If the servant survives the transfer, you only receive a portion over the limit. If not survived, the remainder of the transfer under item 3 is added to this part. Necromancer resistances that have already been taken into account are not reapplied. Associated effects, such as knockback or condition, cannot be carried over.
